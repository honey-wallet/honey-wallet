How to get help with Honey.

Contact us

Email team@honeywallet.org. Tell us which app you use (iPhone, Android or Chrome extension) and what happened. Never send us your recovery phrase, private key or backup password. We will never ask for them.

Common questions

I lost my recovery phrase. Can you recover it?

No. Honey is self-custodial. Your recovery phrase exists only on your device and in any backup you made. We never have a copy, so we cannot recover it or your funds. If you still have access to Honey on a device, back up your phrase now.

How do I back up my wallet?

  • Write down your recovery phrase on paper and keep it somewhere safe and offline.
  • Or, in Honey, create an encrypted backup. You choose a password, and Honey creates a file encrypted with it. Save the file to iCloud Drive or Google Drive from the share sheet. Keep the password somewhere separate. Without it, the file cannot be opened.

How do I restore my wallet on a new device?

Install Honey and choose to import a wallet. Enter your recovery phrase, or pick your backup file and enter its password.

My transaction is stuck or failed.

Check it on the network's block explorer using the link in Honey. Transactions on a blockchain cannot be reversed by us. If a swap or bridge through NEAR Intents did not arrive, email us with the transaction hash.

Report a security issue

Please do not report security issues in public. Report them privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the Honey Wallet repository (Security tab, then "Report a vulnerability"), or email team@honeywallet.org with "Security" in the subject. We acknowledge reports within 3 business days.
