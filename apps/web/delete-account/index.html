Delete your data

For the Honey app and the Honey Wallet Chrome extension, by Honey Wallet.

Before you delete anything: make sure you have your recovery phrase or an encrypted backup and its password. Deleting Honey does not delete your funds, which stay on the blockchain. But without your recovery phrase, nobody, including us, can restore access to them.

Data on your device

Your recovery phrase, keys, wallets, settings and profile are stored only on your device.

  • iPhone or Android: delete the Honey app. This removes its data from the device.
  • Chrome: open chrome://extensions and click Remove on Honey Wallet. This removes its stored data.

Backup files you saved to iCloud Drive or Google Drive are yours. Delete them there if you want them gone.

Data on our servers

To delete your account and the data our servers hold, email team@honeywallet.org with the subject "Delete my data". Include:

  • the email address you signed in with or joined the waitlist with, and/or
  • the wallet addresses you signed in with.

We may ask you to confirm you control the email or wallet, for example by signing a message. We do not need your recovery phrase. Never send it.

What we delete

  • Your waitlist email.
  • Your user ID, linked wallet addresses, sessions and sign-in history.
  • Records of operations you started through our server.
  • Your Firebase sign-in account, if you used Google, Apple or email sign-in.

We aim to complete requests within 30 days and will confirm by email.

What we cannot delete

  • Transactions on public blockchains. They are permanent and public.
  • Data held by third parties under their own policies, such as backups in your iCloud or Google Drive. See our Privacy Policy.
