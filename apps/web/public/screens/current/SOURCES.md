# Current product captures

Captured directly from Honey's native iPhone 17 Pro simulator at 1206 × 2622 on
14 November 2026. Feed, Wallet, Jar and Group Jar are the actual React Native screens in
`apps/mobile`, in light and dark themes. No interface is generated or redrawn.

The app runs in test mode. Balances, returns, people and activity shown are sample
or seeded demo data, not evidence of user traction or investment performance.
Development warning overlays were hidden for capture; the images are otherwise
unedited. The device frame is CSS, not a third-party mockup asset.

Matching files are copied to `deck/public/screens/current/` so the deck can also
build independently. Refresh both copies when changing a capture. The current wallet replaces only the screen inside the original hand-held mockup.
The Jars film retains its original composition and is refreshed separately in
`media/product-flow/`.
